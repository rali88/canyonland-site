Project 2 · Public workforce intelligence

Real public data. Stated rules. Every number traceable to its source.

Project 1 decodes a file we wrote ourselves, which proves technique and nothing else — a synthetic dataset cannot contradict you. This one runs the same lifecycle over 4.86 million payroll records the City of Chicago publishes, so you can re-issue our queries against the City's own API and either land on our numbers or catch us out.
