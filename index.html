Public sector & payroll

Production payroll for a statewide workforce

Prime systems integrator · large public-sector agency

Challenge

Multiple parallel payroll streams—regular, overtime, and separately funded grant payrolls—running on a mainframe platform where a single missed cycle means people are not paid on time.

What we do

Own setup and execution of every payroll type, including supplemental and off-cycle runs; maintain the reporting programs behind them; and carry the platform through fiscal-year transitions, where dataset, calendar, and holiday-table changes must land together and in order.

Outcome

Payroll lands on schedule through every cycle and fiscal-year boundary, with weekly written status reporting to agency and vendor stakeholders.

Environment: z/OS · COBOL · JCL · Roscoe RPF · CA‑Scheduler · Panvalet · Datacom/IDMS

Reliability engineering

Root-cause recovery on a failed payroll cycle

Prime systems integrator · large public-sector agency

Challenge

A production payroll program abended mid-cycle while a concurrent tape-management failure left output datasets uncataloged—two faults presenting as one outage, against a deadline that does not move.

What we do

Isolated the abend to its failing step, separated it from the underlying tape library and retention-key expiry issues, and rebuilt the affected datasets. Delivered a written recovery analysis and resolution plan so the next occurrence is a checklist rather than an investigation.

Outcome

The cycle was recovered and completed on schedule. That failure class is now documented and handled by the operations team without escalation.

Environment: z/OS · COBOL · JCL · CA‑1 tape management · virtual tape · abend analysis

Legacy → analytics

Statutory workforce reporting, moved off legacy tooling

Prime systems integrator · large public-sector agency

Challenge

Compliance and workforce reports—headcount, affirmative-action, and personnel breakdowns—were produced by legacy report-writer jobs that few people could read and nobody could query ad hoc.

What we do

Converted legacy report-writer logic to SQL with output parity line for line, and made the underlying employee data available for direct analysis and pivot-based summaries. Reporting logic became reviewable, testable, and reusable.

Outcome

Workforce and compliance questions that previously required a new report request can now be answered directly against the data.

Environment: EZTrieve · SQL · Datacom · Excel modeling

In progress · AI

Automated documentation for undocumented COBOL estates

Selected for an Oracle cloud innovation program

Challenge

Documentation for a decades-old estate either doesn't exist or describes a system from three decades ago. The people who can fill the gap from memory are retiring.

What we do

Building a generator that reads COBOL source, JCL, and data dictionary definitions and produces maintainer-grade documentation: what a program does, what it reads and writes, and where it sits in the batch flow—grounded in the source rather than invented by the model.

Outcome

In active development, working from a corpus of production COBOL programs, JCL skeletons, and Datacom dictionary definitions.

Environment: Oracle Cloud Infrastructure · LLM tooling · COBOL · JCL · Datacom dictionary

Method

How we deliver

  1. Assess: inventory the estate—programs, jobs, data stores, and who still understands them.
  2. Stabilize: take ownership of the cycle; document failure modes and recovery paths.
  3. Expose: convert reporting logic to SQL so the data can finally be queried.
  4. Modernize: layer BI and AI tooling on top without disturbing the system of record.
  5. Transfer: train the team and hand off runbooks, so the knowledge outlives the contract.
